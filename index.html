About

I'm passionate about digital illustration, UX/UI design, and marketing. At UC Berkeley, I majored in Art Practice and Cognitive Science, which is an interdisciplinary field focused on how minds process information. This combination gave me both creative foundations and analytical skills in programming, data analysis, and user behavior. My work integrates marketing strategy, UX/UI design, graphic design, and front-end web development. These skills are essential in today's landscape where user experience drives business success.
